03From the sales floor

What we see on the lot.

Our sales team walked the front row this week and pulled a quick read on what is moving. As of September 2026, compact and midsize SUVs dominate foot traffic.

Crosstrek and Forester shoppers ask first; Outback shoppers follow close behind. Our team inspected the latest trade-ins as they came in and verified service histories before any pre-owned unit went up for sale.

Last month, a shopper came in convinced they needed a brand-new SUV to get all-wheel drive and a clean history. Our team pulled two Certified Pre-Owned Forester units instead, both with CPO warranty coverage and documented inspections. The shopper drove one home and kept several thousand dollars in the bank. That swap happens more than people expect.

The CPO units that sit one row back often carry more remaining warranty per dollar than a heavily optioned new trim. From the Findlay Subaru sales floor

Here is the contrarian part. Most buyers assume the newest listing online is the best deal. Counterintuitively, price is only the start of the math. We found that pattern again this quarter when we confirmed warranty balances on three CPO Outbacks against comparable new builds.

One more thing our team verified: aggregator photos lag reality. A car shown "available" on a third-party site is sometimes already sold or in service prep. That is why we point shoppers to our own used inventory feed first.

What is the difference between used and Certified Pre-Owned?

A used car may carry remaining factory coverage or sell as-is, depending on the listing. A Certified Pre-Owned Subaru adds a Subaru-backed extended warranty and a manufacturer multi-point inspection.

CPO terms are set by Subaru, so confirm specifics at subaru.com.

How current is the inventory shown online?

Our online inventory updates as vehicles arrive and sell, so it reflects what is actually on the lot. Third-party aggregator sites often lag and may show cars already sold.
